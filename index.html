Treatments, in plain terms

Every plan starts with an examination and a clear explanation of your options and costs.

  • Check-up and cleaning

    Examination, scale and polish, and advice to keep gums and teeth healthy between visits.

    Every 6 months

  • Teeth whitening

    Safe in-clinic whitening for a brighter shade, with guidance on keeping the result.

    1 visit

  • Fillings and repairs

    Tooth-coloured fillings that match your teeth, plus repair of chips and cracks.

    Same day

  • Crowns, bridges and dentures

    Fixed and removable restorations made to match the colour and shape of your own teeth.

    Made to fit

  • Root canal treatment

    Relieves infection and pain while keeping your natural tooth.

    1 to 3 visits

  • Braces and aligners

    Straightening for teenagers and adults, with regular reviews to track progress.

    Months

  • Dental implants

    A fixed replacement for missing teeth that looks and works like your own.

    Planned in stages

  • Gum treatment and surgery

    Treatment for bleeding, swollen or receding gums, including minor gum surgery.

    As needed

  • Veneers and smile design

    E-max and zirconia veneers and crowns that reshape your smile and look natural.

    2 visits
